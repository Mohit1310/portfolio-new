## Lead with clarity
A portfolio should do one job well: make the right person want to talk to you. If your homepage starts with vague claims, visitors have to work too hard to understand your value.

## Prove outcomes, not effort
Each project should explain context, what changed, and what improved. Screenshots are helpful, but outcomes are what build trust.

## Reduce friction in the final step
A simple contact form usually outperforms hidden email links because it removes context switching.

### What to include
- A clear positioning statement.
- Focused case studies.
- One obvious call to action.
